# background / related work — outline

points to cover, roughly in order:

- CGM records continuous glucose, but clinics still compress it into static
  summaries (time in range, GMI, variability indices) -> the *shape* of a meal
  response gets thrown away
- people respond differently to the same food (Zeevi 2015; Hall glucotypes 2018)
  -> individual signal clearly exists
- but is that signal *stable* and *covariate-independent*? open question
- how to measure individuality: ICC (repeatability) + the re-identification idea
  borrowed from biometrics
- CGM features predict diabetes, but watch for circularity (label is defined by
  glucose level)
- gap: no one frames CGM meal responses as re-identification AND ties it to
  diabetes prediction -> that's this paper
